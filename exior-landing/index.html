FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Quick answers to the most common questions about working with EXIOR.

What exactly does EXIOR do?
We sit between emerging technology and the operations that need to adopt it. We evaluate what tech actually fits, design how it integrates with your systems, and implement it end to end — including the change management that makes adoption stick.
How long does a typical engagement last?
Assessments typically run 4-6 weeks, full implementations 3-12 months depending on scope. We define the timeline together after the first discovery call.
Do you work with specific vendors or technologies?
No. We're vendor-neutral. Our only incentive is to recommend the right architecture and partners for your specific context — and we have the depth to actually implement whichever stack you choose.
What's the difference between EXIOR and a traditional consultancy?
Most consultancies deliver a recommendation and disappear. We deliver a recommendation, then we build it, deploy it, train your teams, and stay for the long term. The difference is execution, not just analysis.
How do you measure success on an engagement?
We agree on 3-5 concrete outcomes at the start — operational metrics, time saved, cost reduced, or revenue enabled — and we report against them. If the numbers don't move, we keep working until they do.
