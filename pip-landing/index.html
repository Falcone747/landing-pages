FAQ

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

What can Pip help me with?
Pip helps you budget, track expenses, set savings goals, learn investing basics, and get personalized answers to everyday financial questions — all in one place.
Is Pip free to use?
There's a free tier with the essentials. The Basic plan adds deeper tracking and live support; Premium unlocks personalized advice, advanced tools and priority support.
How does Pip provide personalized advice?
Pip learns from the goals, transactions and questions you share — then adapts its guidance as your situation evolves, while keeping a human in the loop for Premium plans.
Can I track multiple savings goals?
Yes. Basic supports a single goal; Premium lets you track up to five goals side by side, with progress reminders and personalized tips to keep momentum.
Is my financial data safe with Pip?
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, never sold, and only used to give you better guidance. You stay in control of what you share and can delete it any time.
Does Pip offer educational resources?
Yes — a curated library of articles, short tutorials and webinars to sharpen your financial literacy at your own pace, included on every paid plan.
